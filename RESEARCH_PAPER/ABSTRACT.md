***Abstract***—**Modern quantitative wealth management systems consistently face two profound challenges that hinder their adoption in retail and regulated environments. First, they suffer from the Prediction Fallacy, wherein stock return forecasting is treated as a direct proxy for actionable trading decisions. Standard price predictions fundamentally ignore vital real-world constraints such as portfolio covariance, dynamic risk budgeting, strict user-defined limitations, and the compounding penalty of transaction turnover. Second, the deployment of advanced ensemble machine learning models introduces the Black-Box Dilemma. These highly complex models recommend portfolio reallocations without interpretable, deterministic explanations, rendering them unacceptable for regulated applications and eroding investor trust.**

**OptiVest AI addresses both challenges via a comprehensive Adaptive Portfolio Decision Engine (APDE). The core historical evaluation demonstrates the APDE's performance. The platform additionally incorporates a Candidate Discovery Engine (CDE) for asset pre-filtering and a TreeSHAP-based feature attribution module for transparent explanations; these components are evaluated separately from the original historical APDE experiment.**

***Index Terms***—**Machine Learning, Portfolio Optimization, Explainable AI, SHAP, Algorithmic Trading, FinTech.**
