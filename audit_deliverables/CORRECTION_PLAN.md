### 5. Out-of-Sample $R^2$ Terminology (LOW)

- **Affected Location**: Section IV-C, Page 6.
- **Current Manuscript Text**:
  > Formula $R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum (y - \hat{y})^2}{\sum y^2}$ described as out-of-sample $R^2$.
- **Empirical Evidence**:
  - Implemented as `1 - np.sum(errors**2) / np.sum(y**2)` giving $-2.1006\%$.
  - Conventional $R^2$ relative to sample mean gives $-4.4484\%$.
- **Proposed Correction**:
  > Explicitly state that $R^2$ is measured against a zero-forecast benchmark ($R^2_{\text{zero}}$) as formalized by Campbell & Thompson (2008), where a zero-information forecast achieves $0.0\%$.
