# Research Specification — Explainable Adaptive Portfolio Decision Engine (APDE)
**Project:** OptiVest AI  
**Focus:** Portfolio Reallocation Optimization & XAI Feature Attribution  

---

## 1. Abstract & Motivation
Traditional quantitative wealth management systems generally suffer from two core limitations:
1. **The Prediction Fallacy**: Treating stock return forecasting as a direct proxy for trading decisions. Standard price predictions ignore portfolio covariance, portfolio risk budgeting, user constraints, and transaction turnover penalties.
2. **The Black-Box Dilemma**: Deep learning or ensemble ML models recommend trades without interpretable explanations, rendering them unacceptable for regulated FinTech applications and retail investor trust.

**OptiVest AI** addresses both challenges via the **Adaptive Portfolio Decision Engine (APDE)** coupled with **TreeSHAP** feature attribution.

## 2. Mathematical Formulation of APDE

### 2.1 Decision Vector & Portfolio State
Let $\mathbf{w}_0 = [w_{0,1}, w_{0,2}, \dots, w_{0,N}]^T$ represent the vector of current holding weights across $N$ asset universe choices, where $\sum_{i=1}^N w_{0,i} = 1.0$.

The decision variable is the allocation delta vector:
$$\Delta \mathbf{w} = [\Delta w_1, \Delta w_2, \dots, \Delta w_N]^T$$

The post-rebalancing weight vector is:
$$\mathbf{w}_{\text{new}} = \mathbf{w}_0 + \Delta \mathbf{w}$$

### 2.2 Multi-Objective Optimization Problem

$$\max_{\Delta \mathbf{w}} \quad U(\Delta \mathbf{w}) = \mathbb{E}[R(\mathbf{w}_{\text{new}})] - \lambda \cdot \sigma^2(\mathbf{w}_{\text{new}}) - \gamma \cdot \text{Cost}(\Delta \mathbf{w})$$

Where:
- $\mathbb{E}[R(\mathbf{w}_{\text{new}})] = \mathbf{w}_{\text{new}}^T \hat{\mathbf{r}}$, where $\hat{\mathbf{r}}$ is the vector of predicted stock returns generated by the XGBoost prediction engine.
- $\sigma^2(\mathbf{w}_{\text{new}}) = \mathbf{w}_{\text{new}}^T \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{w}_{\text{new}}$, where $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ is the $N \times N$ estimated asset variance-covariance matrix.
- $\lambda > 0$ is the user's risk-aversion coefficient.
- $\gamma > 0$ models transaction cost penalty (brokerage fees + market impact).
- $\text{Cost}(\Delta \mathbf{w}) = \sum_{i=1}^N |\Delta w_i|$.

## 3. Operational Constraints

1. **User Reallocation Cap Constraint ($C_{\text{realloc}}$)**:
   The total fraction of total portfolio wealth shifted out of current holdings must not exceed user-specified limit $C_{\text{realloc}} \in (0, 1]$ (e.g., 10%, 15%):
   $$\sum_{i: \Delta w_i < 0} |\Delta w_i| \le C_{\text{realloc}}$$

2. **Budget Self-Financing Constraint**:
   $$\sum_{i=1}^N \Delta w_i = 0$$

3. **No Short-Selling (Long-Only) Constraint**:
   $$w_{0,i} + \Delta w_i \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$$

4. **Sector Concentration Limit Constraint**:
   $$\sum_{i \in \text{Sector}_k} (w_{0,i} + \Delta w_i) \le S_{\max, k} \quad \forall k$$

## 4. Explainable AI Framework (SHAP Integration)

To provide actionable explanations, APDE applies **TreeSHAP** (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to decompose predicted returns $\hat{r}_i$ into constituent feature contributions:

$$\hat{r}_i = \phi_0 + \sum_{j=1}^M \phi_{i,j}$$

Where:
- $\phi_0$ is the expected base model prediction return.
- $\phi_{i,j}$ is the Shapley value of feature $j$ (e.g., 60-day momentum, RSI, Sharpe ratio, Sector growth) for stock $i$.

### Decision Explanation Output Structure
For every recommended rebalancing trade (e.g., "Sell 5% Stock A, Buy 5% Stock B"), APDE generates structured narrative audit statements:
- **Primary Driver**: High momentum indicator contribution ($\phi_{\text{momentum}} = +0.045$).
- **Secondary Driver**: Sector risk offset ($\phi_{\text{volatility}} = -0.012$).
- **Constraint Compliance**: Trade executed under $15\%$ reallocation cap constraint.
