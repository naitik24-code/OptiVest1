# OptiVest AI Design Map

OptiVest is an explainable portfolio decision system. Its output is a choice among **hold**, **trim**, **replace**, and **cash**, followed by a user-approved paper trade or broker action.

## Runtime pipeline

1. Collect the user's goals, horizon, risk tolerance, liabilities and existing portfolio.
2. Ingest point-in-time prices, fundamentals, liquidity, corporate actions and optional sentiment.
3. Validate freshness, missingness, duplicates, source quality and look-ahead safety.
4. Calculate portfolio health: concentration, volatility, drawdown, diversification and benchmark metrics.
5. Run the Candidate Discovery Engine (CDE) to filter and rank eligible assets.
6. Compare hold, trim, replacement candidates and cash.
7. Run the Adaptive Portfolio Decision Engine (APDE) with long-only, full-investment, position, sector, turnover, cost and tax constraints.
8. Reject weak or unsafe proposals and preserve capital as cash when no alternative clears the evidence bar.
9. Persist a decision card with evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, constraints and rejected alternatives.
10. Require user approval, then simulate paper execution and reconcile actual fills.
11. Monitor drift, thesis failure, model quality and realized outcomes.

## Safe replacement rule

An asset is not replaced solely because of a short-term price fall. A replacement requires a material review trigger, a valid candidate, adequate data quality and a portfolio-level improvement after estimated friction. When an order fails or only partially fills, the system must recalculate from the actual portfolio and may keep the unfilled amount in cash.

## Research requirements

- Point-in-time data and leakage-resistant walk-forward evaluation.
- Baselines including hold, equal-weight, index and risk-parity choices.
- Fees, spreads, slippage, taxes and liquidity constraints.
- Stress tests for crashes, gaps, missing data and stale prices.
- Multiple-testing and backtest-overfitting controls.
- Reproducible manifests that separate verified results from illustrative artifacts.

The API endpoint `GET /api/v1/apde/replacement-options/{portfolio_id}` exposes the hold/replace/cash decision set. The reproducible research figures are generated by `scripts/generate_research_assets.py`, and the upgrade paper is `RESEARCH_PAPER/optivest_upgrade_paper.tex`.
